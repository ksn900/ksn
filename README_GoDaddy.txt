KRISHNA GROUP WEBSITE — GODADDY UPLOAD

Files:
- index.html: complete website (CSS, JavaScript, and product/hero images embedded directly in the file)
- assets/: reserved for future images or files

UPLOAD:
1. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
2. In GoDaddy, open your hosting control panel and launch cPanel File Manager (or the hosting file manager).
3. Open the website's document root, usually public_html.
4. Back up or move any existing index.html before replacing it.
5. Upload index.html into public_html.
6. Visit your domain and refresh the page.

NOTES:
- The current website embeds its images in index.html, so no external image folder is required for the current design.
- The enquiry form opens the visitor's email app and prepares an email to Info@krishnaagrivista.com. It does not send email in the background; for server-side form delivery, connect a form handler or email service.
- The establishment date text has been removed.
